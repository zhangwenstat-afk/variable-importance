# Restricted data: LUCAS 2015

`lucas/lucas.csv` and `lucas/spectra10nm.npz` are derived from the LUCAS 2015 topsoil data
obtained from ESDAC under a personal, non-transferable licence that does not allow
sub-licensing. Do not share or publish these files. Others must request the data at
https://esdac.jrc.ec.europa.eu/content/lucas2015-topsoil-data and rebuild them with
`../data/lucas_spectra.py` and `../data/build_lucas.py`.

Publications using the data must include: "The LUCAS 2015 topsoil dataset used in this
work was made available by the European Commission through the European Soil Data Centre
managed by the Joint Research Centre (JRC), http://esdac.jrc.ec.europa.eu/".
